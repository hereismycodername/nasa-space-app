Adaptation: reduced adult male reference meshes projected into frontal SVG contours,
scaled to a 240 × 500 drawing area. Geometry and anchors are exported together by
scripts/export-anatomy.rs from the native UI data contributed by Asterisksy-zxc.
This reference anatomy is not an individual astronaut's scan or a diagnostic image.

The source mesh headers also retain CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan notices;
the native derived geometry preserves those notices and attribution.
See ui/README.md for provenance and ui/tools/gen_anatomy.py for transformations.

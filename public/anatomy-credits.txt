3D adaptation: original reduced adult male meshes grouped by anatomical concept,
centred and reoriented with Y up and the anterior direction along positive Z,
scaled to a height of two units, simplified for browser performance and converted
to glTF with smooth normals and tissue colours. The local model is
public/anatomy/body.glb, generated by scripts/generate-anatomy-model.py and
scripts/simplify-anatomy-model.mjs. Source hashes, exact original mesh notices and
the list of structures are in /anatomy/body.provenance.json; regeneration and
licence details are in /anatomy/README.md.

2D adaptation: the same reference meshes projected into frontal SVG contours,
scaled to a 240 × 500 drawing area. Geometry and anchors are exported together
by scripts/export-anatomy.rs from the native UI data contributed by Asterisksy-zxc.
This reference anatomy is not an individual astronaut's scan or a diagnostic image.

The source mesh headers also retain CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan notices;
the derived 2D and 3D geometry preserves those notices and attribution and is
additionally shared under those terms:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/
See ui/README.md for provenance and ui/tools/gen_anatomy.py for transformations.
